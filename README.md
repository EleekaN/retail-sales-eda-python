# Retail Sales – Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)

## Problem
Which months, categories, regions and segments drive sales and profit, and do discounts hurt profit?

## Data
Simulated retail sales data (orders, customers, products), about 4240 rows after cleaning. Practice data, not from a real company.

## Steps
1. Loaded and inspected 3 tables
2. Cleaned: removed duplicates, fixed mixed date formats, handled missing values and invalid quantities
3. Merged tables and created month, year and profit-margin columns
4. Analysed trends, categories, regions, products and discounts with charts

## Key findings
1. Sales are highest in October 2024 and lowest in March 2024
2. Technology brings the most sales, but office supplies has the best profit margin of 25.6%.
3. The top 10 products give 54.52% of total profit.
4. Margin falls from 22.1% at no discount to -11.7% at 30% discount.
